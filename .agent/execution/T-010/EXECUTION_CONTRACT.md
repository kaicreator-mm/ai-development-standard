4. **Evidence layers are not PASS** — `IDENTITY_BOUND`, `BEHAVIOR_SUPPORTED`, `INDEPENDENTLY_CHALLENGED` do not imply current Review/Validation PASS or a global score.
5. **Learning routing metadata is non-authoritative** — friction classification/disposition may preserve evidence but cannot mutate ADS or bypass governance.

7. **Friction remains non-normative** — `STANDARD_FRICTION_CANDIDATE` permits `NO_CHANGE` or `MORE_EVIDENCE`; reclassification is an explicit later decision.
8. **No automatic promotion** — count, score, success/failure rate, cost, latency, provider/model label, scheduler/ranking output or heuristic cannot promote friction or authorize a standard change.
9. **ADS evolution enters ordinary governance only** — `ADS_EVOLUTION_CANDIDATE` permits `OPEN_ADS_INTAKE` only, followed by normal Intake→L1→PRD→L2→Task→Review/Validation and integration/release authority.
10. **Ambiguity does not promote** — ambiguous cause/owner/evidence routes to `MORE_EVIDENCE`, not upward classification.
11. **Publication fails closed** — absent explicit `PUBLISHABLE`, evidence stays private/restricted and cannot be copied into public standard evidence.
12. **Publication is orthogonal** — publication class does not change evidence strength, owner authority or promotion status.
13. **Sensitive evidence never publishes** — secrets, credentials, private chain-of-thought and hidden-evaluator/Hidden Validation payloads remain prohibited even when surrounding metadata is publishable.
14. **Reference-first minimization** — refs/digests or minimized non-sensitive summaries are preferred over copied source bodies.
15. **No second evolution authority** — no new evolution lifecycle/schema/database, self-amending telemetry path, numeric promotion engine or standard-change authority is created.

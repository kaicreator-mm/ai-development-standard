> Distinguish standards-source delivery from qualification of a runtime implementing this execution architecture. ADS's own source-only development uses the standards-delivery applicability contract in RELEASE_STANDARD §11.7A. Writing, reviewing, validating or integrating normative text, schemas, examples and contract-conformance tooling MUST NOT require prior deployment or qualification of the orchestration runtime being specified. ### EXECUTION_ARCHITECTURE_STANDARD §14 scope addition

> Sections 14.1–14.4 define the protected-effect contract of a selected Merge Controller implementation and any implementation claiming equivalent controller guarantees. They are not prerequisite runtime-qualification gates for the standards-source manual integration route explicitly defined in RELEASE_STANDARD §11.7A. That route does not claim atomic multi-surface observation, source-issued platform generations, or an atomic source-and-target mutation when its native mechanism does not supply them. This is an explicit subject/mode boundary, not an inference from absent evidence, adoption level or tool choice. Manual integration is subject to its own mandatory pre/post-read, native guard, uncertainty and downstream-hold rules.

### RELEASE_STANDARD §11.7A addition

> For ADS's own standards-source delivery, the declared process executor MUST bind the exact operation, Task/scope authority, source HEAD, target before integration, applicable dependencies/checks/Review/Validation, permission and unresolved-finding inputs. Assurance Plan currentness under EXE_ARCH §29.1 remains mandatory and may be established from durable manually collected facts; it does not require a deployed runtime or confer Gate PASS.
>
> Manual source work MUST have a sole designated admission writer and no incompatible accepted ownership. Recovery MUST either reconcile a real accepted prior binding and its effects, or establish that no binding was accepted. Missing optional historical audit fields alone do not prove invalidity; an unproven prior side effect is not erased by declaring a new session.
>
> Before concern integration the executor MUST fully read the applicable native surfaces and verify current exact HEAD/target, selected Review policy, Validation, native dependencies, checks, permissions and finding dispositions. Use the actual native expected-head guard. Recheck target immediately before requesting integration; if observed target drift invalidates the evidence, stop/recompute. This manual procedure is a bounded sequence, not atomic source/target CAS. A race not prevented by the native mechanism remains an explicit residual limitation.
>
> After the attempt, reconstruct its result from durable native state; bind actual source, target-before and integration SHA/tree and perform the applicable compensating read. Unknown acknowledgement, unexpected source/target, invalidating target drift, new blocker/conflict, incomplete read or failed compensation leaves the integration UNRATIFIED. No downstream READY/DONE/RQ is unlocked until valid current evidence supports a new ratification decision; that decision never backdates a previous failed/unknown admission. An actual unexpected mutation is preserved as an incident, not rewritten as zero mutation or a successful gate.
>
> Source-delivery conformance and runtime qualification are separate subjects. Report REAL_NATIVE_CLAIM_AND_RELEASE_RUNTIME_NOT_PROVEN, REAL_HUMAN_DENY_E2E_NOT_PROVEN and version-required task-meta/Tally not-proven limitations while those capabilities remain unqualified.
